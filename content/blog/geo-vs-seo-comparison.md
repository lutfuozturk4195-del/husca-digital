## The short answer

**SEO gets you found. GEO gets you recommended.** They share the same technical foundation, but they optimize for different output surfaces — a ranked list of links versus a synthesized answer with a handful of citations — which means they reward different content and technical decisions.

## Full comparison

| Dimension | Traditional SEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|
| **Output surface** | Search engine results page (SERP) | AI-generated answer with inline citations |
| **Primary unit optimized** | Keyword / landing page | Entity / claim / answer block |
| **Core success metric** | Ranking position, organic CTR | Citation frequency, share of AI voice |
| **Content structure** | Long-form, keyword-targeted | Answer-first, extractable (lists, tables, definitions) |
| **Trust signal** | Backlink volume, domain authority | Entity consistency, corroboration, structured data |
| **Technical requirements** | Crawlability, page speed, mobile UX | All of SEO's requirements, plus schema depth, `llms.txt`, static/SSR rendering |
| **Update cadence** | Index refresh: weeks to months | Retrieval refresh: often days to weeks |
| **Where it fails silently** | Page ranks but nobody scrolls that far | Page ranks fine, but no AI model ever cites it |

## Where they overlap

Both disciplines depend on:

- A technically healthy, fully crawlable site
- Genuinely authoritative, well-researched content
- A credible entity — consistent naming, real third-party corroboration
- Fast, accessible pages (Core Web Vitals matter to both bots and buyers)

## Where they diverge

SEO rewards **breadth** — ranking for many keyword variants, building topical clusters, accumulating backlinks over time. GEO rewards **precision** — being the single source a model chooses to quote for a specific claim, which depends more on clarity, structure, and corroboration than on keyword coverage.

## The practical takeaway

Don't choose between them. Build the SEO foundation — it's necessary but no longer sufficient — then layer GEO-specific work on top: answer-first restructuring, entity/schema depth, `llms.txt`, and citation tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.
