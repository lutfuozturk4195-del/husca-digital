## The short answer

Ranking well on Google still matters — but it now covers a shrinking slice of the moments that decide a B2B purchase. **AI Overviews answer many informational queries directly on the results page, and a growing share of research starts inside ChatGPT or Perplexity instead of a search box at all.** If your content isn't structured to be extracted and cited by those systems, you can hold page-one rankings and still be absent from the answer your buyer actually reads.

## What changed

Three shifts compound against SEO-only strategies:

- **Zero-click answers grew.** Google's AI Overviews increasingly resolve informational queries — "what is X", "best tool for Y" — without a click, so even a #1 ranking produces no visit.
- **Buyers moved upstream.** Technical buyers now routinely open ChatGPT or Perplexity for "compare X vs Y" or "best [category] for [use case]" questions that used to start as a Google search.
- **Citation replaced ranking as the scarce resource.** A generative answer typically cites a handful of sources. Being source #7 on a results page used to still get clicks; being source #7 an AI model considered but didn't cite gets you nothing.

## What this doesn't mean

It doesn't mean SEO is dead, or that you should abandon keyword-targeted content. Generative engines still lean heavily on the same signals SEO built: crawlable pages, authoritative content, structured data, and reputation across the web. **GEO is additive, not a replacement** — see [What Is GEO?](/blog/what-is-generative-engine-optimization) for the underlying discipline.

## What to do about it

1. **Audit your current AI visibility.** Before changing anything, find out whether ChatGPT and Perplexity already cite you, and for which queries — [run a free AI Citation & Visibility Audit](/#audit).
2. **Restructure your highest-intent pages to be answer-first.** Lead with a direct, quotable answer, then support it with detail.
3. **Fix the technical gaps that block extraction** — thin structured data, JS-only rendering, missing `llms.txt`, weak entity signals.
4. **Treat citation frequency as a metric**, not just organic sessions and rankings.
